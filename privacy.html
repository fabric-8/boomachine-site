BOO MACHINE

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Boo Machine does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Information on your device

The app stores your selected disc and whether the tutorial has been shown locally. Your selected repeating sounds stay in memory and disappear when the app closes. Sounds and artwork are bundled with the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Boo Machine reads Apple's verified transaction history to provide and restore access to purchased discs. We do not receive your payment information. Apple's handling of transactions is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove its local preferences. If you reinstall, use Restore purchases to recover an existing Unlock all purchase. Purchases remain subject to Apple's account and transaction policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@fabric8.de.
