Page statistics, without cookies

To know how many people visit the site and which parts of the page work, boomachine.app counts visits with a small script of its own. It runs only at boomachine.app, on the same ALL-INKL servers in Germany. No analytics service or other third party is involved, and nothing is stored on your device: no cookies, no local storage, no identifiers.

When you open a page, and when you do certain things on it, your browser sends a short message to the server. It contains:

  • what happened: the page was opened, stayed open for 10 seconds, was scrolled a quarter, half, three quarters or all of the way, one of the three story sections came into view, the intro finished, the disc was changed, you signed up for the launch notification (only that it happened and from which of the page's forms, never the address) or confirmed it, the App Store link, the press kit or the email address was clicked;
  • the page's address;
  • the domain of the website that linked to it (only the domain, not the full address) and campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) if the link had them;
  • whether the browser window is phone, tablet or desktop sized, and the two-letter code of your browser's language;
  • a random number made up for that one page view, which is not kept in your browser.

The server adds the time and the browser family (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge or other), which it reads from your browser's user agent.

To count unique visitors without recognising anyone, the server computes a shortened hash from your IP address, your browser's user agent and a random secret that is replaced by a new one with the first visit of each day, the old one being overwritten. Only the hash is stored with the events. Your IP address and your full user agent are not stored. Because the secret changes daily, the same visitor gets a different hash each day, so visits cannot be linked across days or traced back to an IP address. Requests from known bots are discarded, and each hash is limited to a number of events per day.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the script sends nothing at all. Blocking the script with a content blocker changes nothing else on the site. The events are kept as files on my web space at ALL-INKL, which processes them on my behalf under the data processing agreement mentioned above, and are deleted after 400 days. Only I look at them, and only as totals (for example visitors per day, how many reached the story, how many clicked "Join the beta").

Legal basis: Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is in knowing how many people visit the site and which parts of the page work, so that I can improve it.

Launch and new-disc emails

If you enter your email address in the form on this site, I use it for one thing only: to tell you by email when Boo Machine launches on the App Store and when new discs for it come out. You get no other mails, and your address is never passed on, sold or used for advertising.

Double opt-in. After you send the form, a confirmation mail goes to the address you entered. Only when you click the link in it is the address added to the list. If you do not confirm, the unconfirmed entry is deleted automatically after 7 days.

What is stored:

  • your email address;
  • the time you signed up, the times confirmation mails were sent, and the time you confirmed;
  • which version of the sign-up text you agreed to (with a checksum of its exact wording) and which form on the page you used;
  • a keyed hash of the IP address you signed up from and of the one you confirmed from. The IP address itself is not stored, and the hash cannot be turned back into it without a secret key that never leaves the server;
  • a random code for your personal confirmation and unsubscribe links.

To stop abuse of the form, the server also counts sign-up attempts per network for 10 minutes, under a keyed hash of the IP address; these counters are deleted with the next sign-up after that.

Legal basis. Sending the emails is based on your consent, Article 6 (1) (a) GDPR. Keeping the record of your sign-up and confirmation serves to demonstrate that consent (Article 7 (1) GDPR); the legal basis for that is Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR, my legitimate interest being to be able to prove that you asked for the emails.

Where. The list is kept as files on my web space at ALL-INKL.COM in Germany, and the emails are sent from ALL-INKL's mail servers in Germany, which process the data on my behalf under the data processing agreement mentioned above. No newsletter service or other third party is involved.

How long. Until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete your address. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 7 days. When you unsubscribe, your address and your links are deleted at once; what remains is a keyed hash of the address with the date you left, which cannot be read as an address and only serves to show that you withdrew your consent and to make sure no further mails reach you. If you sign up again later, it is replaced by a new sign-up.

The Boo Machine app

Boo Machine is an offline soundboard for iPhone. It processes no personal data:

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".
  • No network use except purchases. The app makes no network requests of its own and has no server behind it. All sounds and artwork are bundled with the app and play offline. The only connection is made by Apple's StoreKit framework when you view, buy or restore the "Unlock all" purchase.
  • No device permissions. The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, location or anything else.

Purchases through Apple

The optional "Unlock all" purchase is made through Apple's App Store using Apple's StoreKit. Apple is the controller for the transaction: Apple handles your Apple Account, payment details and receipts, and I never see them. The app only reads Apple's verified, on-device record of your purchase to unlock the discs and to restore the purchase on a new device. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Apple provides me with aggregate sales reports that do not identify you.

Settings stored on your device only

The app remembers which disc you last loaded and whether the introductory tutorial has been shown. These two settings are stored in the app's own preferences on your iPhone, are never transmitted anywhere, and are removed when you delete the app. Sounds you set to repeat stay in memory only while the app is open.

No sharing

No data is shared with me or with anyone else, because none is collected.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is rated for age in the App Store according to Apple's questionnaire; it contains spooky sounds and Halloween artwork but no gore, no chat and no external links except Apple's purchase sheet.

  • objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21). Where processing rests on Article 6 (1) (f), you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation, and I will stop unless there are compelling legitimate grounds.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@boomachine.app. The page statistics cannot be connected to you, so I cannot find your entries in them (Article 11 GDPR); to object to them, turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser, and nothing is sent from then on.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your residence, workplace or the place of the alleged infringement. A list of all German supervisory authorities is published by the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection.

No automated decision-making or profiling takes place. You are not obliged to provide any personal data to use the site or the app; only an email needs an address to be answered.
