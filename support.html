BOO MACHINE

A little help with the haunting.

Getting started

Tap a pad to play a sound. Tap several to layer them. Eject opens the collection. Expand a disc to see its sounds and load it.

Repeating sounds

Tap Repeat, then tap pads to add or remove independently repeating sounds. Atmospheres sustain; rhythms and stingers return after their own pauses. Turn Repeat off to clear the selection. Hold Repeat to silence everything and clear.

No sound?

Check your iPhone's media volume and your Bluetooth or AirPlay output. Boo Machine plays in Silent Mode. Calls, audio interruptions and leaving the app stop and clear repeating sounds.

Restoring a purchase

Open The collection and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, and check your internet connection. Unlock all is a single non-consumable purchase covering all pads and discs, including future discs added to the app.

Contact support

Email hello@fabric8.de. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Never send your Apple Account password or payment details.
