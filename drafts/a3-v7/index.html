One key, one creepy sound.

Wind, then Whisper. Every key on the Haunted house disc is a scene: a draught under the door, a voice that is not quite there, a knock nobody answers. Six of its twelve sounds are free. Tap one and the room changes.

Loop the room.

Hit Repeat and the machine keeps the scene alive. Layer Wind, Whisper and Knock, put the phone down, and let the house breathe on its own for as long as the party lasts. Tap a key again to take it out of the loop.

Change the disc.

Eject, and the collection slides up: Haunted house, Creature feature, The coven. Load a disc and the whole player turns with it, from the artwork behind the glass to the twelve keys. One purchase unlocks every sound on every disc, and future discs too. No ads, no subscription.
