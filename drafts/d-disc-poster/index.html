The machine Four controls, nothing to learn
Tap
Twelve pads on the disc in the tray. Press one, it plays.
Layer
Press more. Wind under whispers under footsteps — they stack into a room.
Repeat
Turn Repeat on and the scene runs by itself. Hold it down to silence everything at once.
Eject
Eject swaps the disc. Three to choose from, each with its own twelve.
