Iteration 8 — the uncovered track is a furnace window

  1. A3 · V2h — V2g with the slide's fire rebuilt after the note "I want the area BEHIND the slider to be filled with fire". The space the knob has uncovered, from the left cap to the knob's edge, is now filled the whole height of the track with a simulated fire: a Doom-style heat field at 1.6 px cells, an ember bed along the floor, tongues climbing the whole height and licking ~18 px past the rail, dark valleys between them, a fine flicker over it, sparks leaving the rail and a bloom outside it that grows with the travel. At 10 % it is a narrow slot of fire, at 85 % almost the whole track is an inferno and the room light rises to ~1.8; the knob still drags a tongue of it. On release the fire collapses toward the left cap and burns out from the floor; on completion the whole track burns and flares white. Idle: a few embers in the seam at the left cap. Reduced motion: one static frame of the fire. Same track, same knob, same label, same dust

Iteration 7 — the knob trails a flame

  1. A3 · V2g — V2f with the slide rebuilt. The boiler hatch is gone: no blur, no bars, no segmented interior. The track is crisp scorched metal with one faint red edge light, and the knob you drag trails a FLAME — a particle trail on a canvas, anchored to the knob's left edge and left behind in its wake, white-hot at the knob and cooling through yellow, orange and deep red to smoke at the tail. The knob's speed drives it: still, it is a small idling lick; dragged fast, it streams the length of the track, throws embers over the rail and leaves the floor of the track glowing where it has been, which cools over about a second and a half. Half again as much dust in the air, each mote with a slow depth twinkle of its own; and the room light comes up more gently than it did (1 → 1.6)
