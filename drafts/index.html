Iteration 16 — smooth

  1. A3 · V5 — v4 made cheap and deterministic after the note "it's janky; the hands get stuck or overlap; the phone is too small; make the bar a floating island". The STAGE is one scroll-driven timeline: the stage progress is the only clock, the hand morphs over p .30–.40 and .63–.73 as a function of that progress (WebGL displacement, canvas sized to the phone, DPR ≤ 2), the display crossfades and the copy follow the same timeline (scrub 0.4) — scrolling back and forth can never leave two hands overlapping. Only the clip of the current state plays; the others are display:none until just before their crossfade. No blend mode on any viewport-sized layer (the grain is an alpha tile moved by transform), no blur filter, the scrubbed wrappers and the bulb promoted, ScrollTrigger refreshes once when fonts + images are in and never during a scroll. THE PHONE IS BIG: on the phones3 set (1856x2320, the rails clean under every hand) the frame spans 100svh on desktop, capped at 56 % of the row (the display 66svh at 1440x900, 71svh at 1440x700), full width minus 24 px on a phone with the copy above; the clips re-encoded at 906x1970 from the native captures. THE HEADINGS are the rendered Jacquard 12 tube signs (one line, one size, placed on the text's ink, flicker as opacity). THE ISLAND: a centred 420 x 52 capsule, dark, a 1 px red edge light, slides down when the slider leaves. Hero, slider, support, footer, legal, press kit, reduced motion unchanged

  1.
